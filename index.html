Research Focus

Theoretical Methodology

Leveraging information-theoretic metrics (e.g., Fisher information and entropy parameters) to reason about model uncertainty, guide safe system identification, and handle model drift in real time.

Algorithmic Frameworks

Designing modular, low-level orchestration architectures that embed physics-based kinematic models directly into learning loops to close the sim-to-real gap.
