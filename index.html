Research Focus

"Be the change that you want to see in the world."

Core Objective

Integrating learning methods with classical control and motion planning to build autonomous systems that are adaptive, safe, and mathematically grounded.

Theoretical Methodology

Leveraging information-theoretic tools such as Fisher information and entropy-based criteria to reason about uncertainty, support safe identification, and maintain robustness under drift.

Algorithmic Framework

Building modular orchestration architectures that embed physics-based kinematics inside learning loops to improve sample efficiency and close the sim-to-real gap.
