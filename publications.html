Publications

Peer-reviewed work in robotics simulation and autonomous systems.

Selected contributions focused on system modeling, sim-to-real transfer, and optimization for mobile robots.

IEEE ICRA 2024

Conference Paper

Simulation Modeling of Highly Dynamic Omnidirectional Mobile Robots Based on Real-World Data

M. Wiedemann, O. Ahmed, A. Dieckhofer, R. Gasoto, S. Kerner

Developed a data-driven simulation modeling workflow based on motion-capture telemetry to reduce sim-to-real discrepancy in holonomic logistics platforms.

IEEE CASE 2025

Conference Paper

Automated Tuning of Non-Differentiable Rigid Body Simulation Models for Wheeled Mobile Robots

M. Wiedemann, O. Ahmed, M. Hatwar, et al.

Formulated a black-box optimization pipeline that automatically tunes rigid-body simulation parameters for differential, Ackermann, and omnidirectional robots using real-world motion data.
