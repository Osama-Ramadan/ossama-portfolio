Research Statement

Toward reliable and adaptive autonomy under uncertainty.

My work integrates model-based control, machine learning, and system identification to design robotic systems that are both high-performing and verifiably stable in real-world deployment.

Current Research Direction

Bridging classical and data-driven methods in a rigorous, implementation-oriented framework.

Uncertainty-Aware Identification

Using information-theoretic indicators (Fisher information and entropy-based criteria) to guide safe model identification and detect model drift in operational robotics systems.

Model Calibration for Sim-to-Real

Automating calibration of non-differentiable rigid-body simulation models from telemetry, improving transfer reliability for control and planning policies.

Learning-Enabled Control Pipelines

Embedding kinematic and dynamic priors into learning loops to increase sample efficiency while maintaining formal safety and stability constraints.

PhD Interests

Research problems I want to investigate during doctoral studies.

Robust Adaptive MPC

Combining adaptive identification with robust MPC formulations to handle changing system dynamics and uncertainty bounds online.

Structure-Preserving Learning

Designing hybrid model classes that preserve controllability and passivity constraints while learning residual dynamics from data.

Principled Sim-to-Real Metrics

Defining measurable discrepancy metrics between simulated and real trajectories to drive automated simulator tuning and policy transfer quality.

Safety in Learning Loops

Integrating invariant sets, safety filters, and confidence-aware exploration mechanisms into learning-based autonomous control stacks.
